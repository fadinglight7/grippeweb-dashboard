GrippeWeb Dashboard

Explore acute respiratory illness (ARE) and influenza-like illness (ILI) estimates in Germany.

About this dashboard

This dashboard presents data from the RKI GrippeWeb weekly report. Use the tabs to explore incidence trends over time, compare age groups, and examine regional differences across Germany.

How to use the dashboard

Incidence Over Time: Compare weekly incidence across selected seasons for Germany as a whole.

Incidence by Age Group: Compare weekly incidence for different age groups within a selected season.

Regional Incidence Map: Select a season, week, illness category, and age group to explore regional incidence.

Incidence Over Time

Compare weekly nationwide incidence across seasons. Select one or more seasons to display a separate line for each season.

Data are filtered to Region = Bundesweit and Altersgruppe = 00+. The horizontal axis follows the epidemiological season: weeks 40–53, followed by weeks 1–39 where available.

Incidence by Age Group

Compare the weekly nationwide incidence of different age groups within a selected season.

Data are filtered to Region = Bundesweit. The age groups shown here are 0–4, 5–14, 15–34, 35–59, and 60+.

Regional Incidence Map

Explore the distribution of incidence across the four regions. Use the filters and slider to change the displayed week.
