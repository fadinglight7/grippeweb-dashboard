GrippeWeb Dashboard

Interactive visualization of respiratory illness incidence in Germany

Welcome to GrippeWeb

This dashboard presents estimates of respiratory illness incidence in Germany using data from the GrippeWeb surveillance system. It allows you to explore how respiratory illness patterns change over time and how they vary across age groups and regions.

Use the tabs above to explore the different visualizations. You can compare seasons, examine age-specific patterns, and investigate regional differences using the available filters. The charts and map update according to your selections.

Reading the map

What do the colours represent?

Each region is coloured according to its incidence for the selected disease, season, and epidemiological week. Use the legend to interpret the ranges. In general, darker orange and brown shades indicate higher incidence; consult the legend for the exact values.
