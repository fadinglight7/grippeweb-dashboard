GrippeWeb Dashboard

Interactive visualization of respiratory illness incidence in Germany

Welcome to GrippeWeb

This dashboard presents estimates of respiratory illness incidence in Germany using data from the GrippeWeb surveillance system.

Explore the dashboard using the tabs above:

  • Incidence Over Time: Explore changes in nationwide incidence across seasons.
  • Incidence by Age Group: Compare incidence patterns across five age groups.
  • Regional Incidence Map: Compare incidence across regions of Germany.

Use the available filters to select seasons, weeks, diseases, and age groups where applicable. The visualizations update to reflect your selections.

Reading the map

What do the colours represent?

Each region is coloured according to its incidence for the selected disease, season, and epidemiological week. Use the legend to interpret the ranges. In general, darker orange and brown shades indicate higher incidence; consult the legend for the exact values.

Understanding the chart

What am I looking at?

Understanding age groups

What does incidence mean?

Incidence here is expressed per 100,000 inhabitants. It helps compare populations of different sizes. It is not the raw number of people who became ill, and it does not tell us an individual's personal risk.
