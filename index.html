GrippeWeb Dashboard

Interactive visualization of respiratory illness incidence in Germany

GrippeWeb

This dashboard presents incidence estimates from the GrippeWeb surveillance system for respiratory illnesses in Germany.

Use the tabs above to explore the data from different perspectives.

  • Incidence Over Time – compare nationwide incidence across different seasons.
  • Incidence by Age Group – examine nationwide incidence across five different age groups.
  • Regional Incidence Map – explore incidence across German regions by season and calendar week.

Use the available filters to change the disease, season, calendar week, or age group, depending on the visualization.
