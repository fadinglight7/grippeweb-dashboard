GrippeWeb Dashboard

Interactive visualization of respiratory illness incidence in Germany

GrippeWeb

This dashboard presents incidence estimates from the GrippeWeb surveillance system for respiratory illnesses in Germany.

Use the tabs above to explore the data from different perspectives.

  • Regional Map – explore incidence across German regions by season and calendar week.
  • Incidence – compare nationwide incidence across different seasons.
  • Age Groups – examine nationwide incidence across different age groups.

The filters can be used to change the disease, season, calendar week, or age group depending on the visualization.
